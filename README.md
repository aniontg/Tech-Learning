# Technology & Learning Blog

A modern educational blog exploring how technology is transforming the way students learn.

## About the Project

**Technology & Learning** is a blog website focused on the relationship between education and technology. The project explores how digital tools are changing the learning experience for students and creating new opportunities for education.

The blog covers topics such as online learning, educational applications, digital resources, artificial intelligence, and the future of education.

##  Features

* Clean and responsive blog layout
* Navigation menu
* Featured blog content
* Educational and technology-focused articles
* Images and visual content
* Responsive design for different screen sizes
* Interactive elements
* Structured and reusable components

##  Topics Covered

* Technology in Education
* Online Learning
* Educational Apps
* Artificial Intelligence and Learning
* Digital Learning Resources
* The Future of Education
* Challenges of Technology in Education

## Technologies Used

* **HTML5** – For the structure and semantic content
* **CSS3** – For styling, layout, and responsive design
* **JavaScript** – For interactive functionality
* **React** – For building reusable and dynamic user-interface components
